2. TRANSFORMATION & SELECTION PIPELINE
===============================================================================

The raw lexical dump was deterministically filtered, normalized, and indexed by the
PDF Language Companion build pipeline (scripts/build-dictionary.mjs):
- Filtered to German headwords with English glosses
- Cleaned grammatical notations and redundant definitions
- Structured multi-sense homographs with gender and plural tags
- Compiled full inflection-to-canonical-lemma mapping index
